INTERPRETATION

The direction matters.

Positive error means remaining life was overestimated. A maintenance decision based on that number could be delayed. Negative error means the prediction was conservative and could prompt early replacement.

Short histories may contain little visible degradation. These examples expose an error pattern; they do not prove its cause.

LIMITS & NEXT EXPERIMENT

Evidence has a boundary.

FD001 is simulated, with one operating condition and one fault mode. Targets are capped at 125 cycles. Seed spread is a stability indicator, not a calibrated confidence interval.

Next: freeze the history model and evaluate on independent data and broader conditions, with a matched baseline.
